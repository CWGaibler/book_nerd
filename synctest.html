Setup needed: paste your Firebase settings

In the Firebase console, copy the settings block for your web app (it starts with const firebaseConfig = {) and paste all of it here. It is not a secret.

A Home Screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari, so it will ask once more the first time you open it that way.

Step 1: Sign in

Step 2: Save a note, then look at your other devices

Type anything and save it. It should appear on every device where you are signed in.

What to check (about 10 minutes)

  1. Sign in on this device.
  2. Save a note, then open this page on another device and sign in. The note should be there within a few seconds.
  3. Save a note on the second device and watch it appear here without touching anything.
  4. Offline test: turn on Airplane Mode, save a note (it will say "Saving…"), turn Airplane Mode off. It should become "Saved" and appear on your other device.
  5. Close the page completely, reopen it. You should still be signed in.
  6. iPhone and iPad: Share, Add to Home Screen, open it from the icon. "Opened as" should say Home Screen app. Sign in there and repeat steps 2 to 5.
